About

I’m a fourth-year electrical and computer engineering student at the University of Toronto. I’m enthusiastic about taking what I learn and using it where it can have an impact.

The work I like best is hands-on: circuit analysis, circuit design, and checking that a real circuit behaves the way the analysis said it would. Courses in energy systems and power-system protection, and a capstone on load forecasting, are where I am putting that to work. I also build in software when the problem needs it.
